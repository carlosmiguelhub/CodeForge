# SQWeb Decision Log

Status: Active  
Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Decision policy

- This log records product and architecture decisions, their status, and rationale.
- “Approved by delegation” means the product owner explicitly authorized the architect to choose the necessary conservative baseline.
- A superseding decision must reference the earlier entry and document migration impact.
- Implementation discoveries do not silently change approved behavior.
